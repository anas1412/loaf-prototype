How it's made

Loaf Kitchen is a folder of HTML files built with Loaf. There's no server and no database: it's hosted for free on GitHub Pages, and everything you save stays in your browser.

The whole shopping list

This is all the code behind the list on the Shopping page, apart from some classes for the look.

<loaf-form name="shopping">
  <input name="item" placeholder="Add an item">
  <button>Add</button>
</loaf-form>

<loaf-list name="shopping">
  <input toggle="bought">
  <span edit="item"></span>
  <button remove><loaf-icon name="x"></loaf-icon></button>
</loaf-list>

What each part does

  • <loaf-form> saves what you type.
  • <loaf-list> shows everything saved, newest first.
  • toggle, edit and remove tick, change and delete items.
  • Buttons, cards and the 35 themes come from daisyUI.

Private by default

Your recipes and lists are saved in this browser only. Nobody else can see them.

Tabs stay in sync

Open the shopping list in two tabs and tick something off: the other tab updates too.

Works on your phone

Add it to your home screen and take your list to the shop.
